# Domain rules

These are the rules the program enforces now, grouped by feature. Rules confirmed by the product owner are marked as such; release-by-release history is in CHANGELOG.md and docs/history/.

## Authority and approved wording

Legal wording, ordinance interpretation, court schedules, numbering, authority, seasonal policy and language approval belong to the division. The mandatory-appearance hold remains enforced in Writer and helper until approved distinct defendant instructions are supplied. Historical frozen reprints remain unchanged. Current approved document and court wording (notice blocks, document bodies, court details, copies, instructions and contact) is data in app/assets/approved_language.json; change it only with the division's language approval (L5). Issued documents keep their frozen copies.

## Issued records and identities

Preserve issued snapshots, event/item/root/visit identities, action chronology, correction lineage, withdrawals versus compliance, deadline limits, initial notice/citation atomic publication and legacy readers. Device saving is not issuance; a filed marker is not PDF proof; an Outlook draft is not delivery. A pending publication confirms its retained request before another action. Every document keeps the authoring version it was issued with; earlier versions are unchanged and corrections render in the source's format.

## Inspection import

Inspection imports use the unchanged fixed read-only SQL query or division SSRS Excel. Both paths use the inspection-type/status matrix in accela_import.js:

| Inspection type | Statuses that decide a document |
| --- | --- |
| Initial Inspection | Official Notice, Citation, No Occupancy, Abatement Summary, Abatement Non-Summary |
| Reinspection | CA Referral / City Attorney Referral, Compliance, Citation, Extension Processing, No Fee Extension Processing, No Occupancy, No Fee No Occupancy, Reinspection, No Fee Reinspection (Reinspection Notice) |
| CA Reinspection | Compliance, Non Compliance |

Completed and all unlisted results are refused. Never listed (owner rule): Scheduled, Pending, Completed, Inspection and No Violation results for every type, Reinspection and No Fee Reinspection results for every type except a Reinspection (owner request after R64: for a Reinspection they are listed and lead to the Reinspection Notice), Initial Inspection No Entry, Assist inspections, undated rows and inspections already used on the case. The refresh of an unissued draft's or worksheet's own inspection is not a use. Other type/status pairs are listed, greyed and not selectable, with their reason. Unknown statuses, unsupported types, missing actual dates and future dates cannot authorize an import. Blank times remain blank. A result status is not evidence that Document Writer issued a document; shared record identities independently prevent reuse. Existing unfinished work opens through Continue existing case. Several eligible results require an explicit choice. Snow Removal record category retains the MGO 10.28 route.

**Reinspection Notice (owner request after R64).** A Reinspection or No Fee Reinspection result of a Reinspection opens the worksheet and leads to a `reinspectionNotice` document: a primary follow-up of the recorded reinspection, header "REINSPECTION - Building Inspection Official Notice", the approved paragraph in `approved_language.json` (`documentBodies.reinspectionNotice`, identical for both statuses), corrected items ruled through with their confirmation date by the existing sentence and every other item listed outstanding. It never assigns, moves or removes a due date: each item prints the due date it already had (corrected items keep theirs), the date is shown as text and is not editable, past dates print as assigned, and the helper refuses an issued document whose due dates differ from its source or that has no outstanding item (`_validate_followup_outcomes`). A correction of a Reinspection Notice keeps every due date as well (`_validate_reinspection_notice_correction`). It is outside the referral workflow (refused on a case with the City Attorney), sets the case status Reinspection in both case projections and Reports, and is created only from the imported status. Authoring version 16 is stamped on the draft; the authoring-17 identity belongs to the release the owner assigns.

**Bundled order language approval (owner decision 2026-10-31).** The bundled library entries that were marked draft and have an ordinance section (123, including three whose section was filled in from the supplied MGO text and the library's sibling entries: Unsecured Vacant Building 27.085(2)(a), Smoke Alarms 27.05(2)(w), Plumbing work without required approval 18.08) are approved for selection as standard entries (`p` is no longer `draft`; `approval` records the decision; the catalog manifest `draft` flag follows). No catalog review event is recorded for them, so they appear under Approved and standard and not under the default Approved language scope. Seven entries remain draft with no section (both carbon monoxide alarm entries, Occupancy Exceeds Permitted Use, Required permit inspections, Historic exterior work approval, Sleeping room without required egress, Unapproved room use); the picker and the helper refuse them until the division supplies a section and records a review. Citations in the library carry no trailing period after a numbered subdivision (for example 27.05(2)(g)1); the pricing code adds it back when it matches the schedule.

## Notices, items and deadlines

New notices begin with no automatic item deadline. At most three distinct outstanding-item dates are permitted by the existing rules. Physical visits and their item findings are distinct from document actions. No-access findings are not compliance. Withdrawn items retain their reason and identity. Private language edits survive removal/reselection; reviewed-version adoption is explicit; issued wording stays frozen.

## Extensions

Owner rules: new Best Interest and Vacancy Extensions cover every uncorrected item with no selection, no safety confirmation and no signer block. Best Interest due dates follow the normal Extension rules (the worksheet's Next due date, editable in Violation items). Vacancy prints no due date for uncorrected items and needs a review inspection at least seven days after mailing. Drafts in earlier formats keep them; only explicit adoption moves an unissued Best Interest draft to the current format. The existing Best Interest safety-exclusion checkbox remains for authoring-15 drafts, which need that explicit confirmation but no separate safety-basis narrative. Older formats and other families retain their requirements.

## Histories and referrals

Owner rules: show the inspections that actually observed each item, beginning at that item's first observation, including later-added items. Days Outstanding stops at correction; outstanding items end at their latest recorded observation. Carried compliance is not a new observation. No date is inferred from mailing/issuance or unrelated case visits. New referral, referral-update and compliance documents print every note under the violation, corrected items included. Compliance letters retain item-specific observation histories and explicit Abate values. A referred case allows only Update for Case Referral or Compliance Letter; corrections of earlier documents remain possible.

## Citations and pricing

Required citation copies remain Court, Agency, Inspector and Defendant, with approved distinct content; previews and PDFs must contain all four distinct copies per charge (owner rule). Pricing preserves determined locks, counted history, approved basis provenance, required measurements and unchanged-correction prices. Correction money formatting never changes stored units or pricing. Manual authority is separate from the mandatory wording hold. Snow uses its existing site/season rules and no ordinary repair deadline. Do not change policy to bypass a readiness check.

**Violator contact (owner request after R66).** A Copies entry may have the type Violator (`ccList` entry with `kind: "Violator"`, at most one per case). On every non-snow document it replaces the property owner as the main contact in the mailing address block and as the citation defendant and address; a snow removal case (`snow-10.28`) ignores it. The owner is printed under CC by default and the case flag `copyOwner: false` removes it (the flag is stored only when turned off, so re-ticking the box leaves no correction change). With a Violator set, the mailing-block readiness checks lead to Recipients. The Violator is the pricing respondent for repeat offenses and the registered-agent rule (`defendantKey` in citation_policy.js, mirrored by `defendant_key` in helper/citation_pricing.py); the case summary and Reports keep the owner key (`respondentKey`). Frozen documents capture the whole case, so the Violator and flag freeze with each issued document. No case-record version was added: the helper rewrites saved cases at schema 5, so installs that predate the feature print the Violator as an ordinary copy and use the owner as defendant until updated (acceptance SVIO-4). The mailing block is read-only while a Violator is set.

## Reports

Reports accepts only verified case bytes matching summary sourceRevision, and only a summary whose reportable fields equal the helper's projection of that verified case (helper/case_projection.py, the exact mirror of summarize()); legacy, stale, duplicate, unreadable, unproven or non-matching projections remain visible as excluded coverage. The case file is the authority: a rebuild rewrites summaries, never cases or integrity history. Current table, CSV, workbook and report print use the same row ordering. Cached review metadata is labeled and cannot bypass publication validation.
