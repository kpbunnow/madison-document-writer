# Domain rules

Legal wording, ordinance interpretation, court schedules, numbering, authority, seasonal policy and language approval belong to the division. The mandatory-appearance hold remains enforced in Writer and helper until approved distinct defendant instructions are supplied. Historical frozen reprints remain unchanged. Current approved document and court wording (notice blocks, document bodies, court details, copies, instructions and contact) is data in app/assets/approved_language.json; change it only with the division's language approval (L5). Issued documents keep their frozen copies.

Preserve issued snapshots, event/item/root/visit identities, action chronology, correction lineage, withdrawals versus compliance, deadline limits, initial notice/citation atomic publication and legacy readers. Device saving is not issuance; a filed marker is not PDF proof; an Outlook draft is not delivery. A pending publication confirms its retained request before another action.

Inspection imports use the unchanged fixed read-only SQL query or division SSRS Excel. Both paths use the inspection-type/status matrix in app_core.js: Initial Inspection permits Official Notice, Citation, No Occupancy and the two Abatement results; Reinspection permits CA Referral/City Attorney Referral, Compliance, Citation, Extension Processing and No Occupancy; CA Reinspection permits Compliance and Non Compliance. Completed and all unlisted results are refused. Scheduled, Pending, Assist, unknown statuses, unsupported types, missing actual dates and future dates cannot authorize an import. Blank times remain blank. A result status is not evidence that Document Writer issued a document; shared record identities independently prevent reuse. Existing unfinished work opens through Continue existing case. Several eligible results require an explicit choice. Snow Removal record category retains the MGO 10.28 route.

New notices begin with no automatic item deadline. At most three distinct outstanding-item dates are permitted by the existing rules. Physical visits and their item findings are distinct from document actions. No-access findings are not compliance. Withdrawn items retain their reason and identity. Private language edits survive removal/reselection; reviewed-version adoption is explicit; issued wording stays frozen.

Pricing preserves determined locks, counted history, approved basis provenance, required measurements and unchanged-correction prices. Manual authority is separate from the mandatory wording hold. Snow uses its existing site/season rules and no ordinary repair deadline. Do not change policy to bypass a readiness check.

Reports accepts only verified case bytes matching summary sourceRevision, and only a summary whose reportable fields equal the helper's projection of that verified case (helper/case_projection.py, the exact mirror of summarize()); legacy, stale, duplicate, unreadable, unproven or non-matching projections remain visible as excluded coverage. The case file is the authority: a rebuild rewrites summaries, never cases or integrity history. Current table, CSV, workbook and report print use the same row ordering. Cached review metadata is labeled and cannot bypass publication validation.

R60 referral rule confirmed by the product owner: show inspections that actually observed each item, beginning at that item’s first observation, including later-added items. Days Outstanding stops at correction; outstanding items end at their latest recorded observation. Carried compliance is not a new observation. No date is inferred from mailing/issuance or unrelated case visits. Required citation copies remain Court, Agency, Inspector and Defendant, with approved distinct content.

R62 owner rules (Development 3): imports follow the type/status table with No Fee Extension Processing and No Fee No Occupancy; Scheduled, Pending, Completed, Inspection, No Violation, Reinspection, No Fee Reinspection, Initial Inspection No Entry, Assist, undated and already-used inspections are hidden (the refresh of an unissued draft's own inspection is not used); other pairs are listed unavailable. Authoring-16 Best Interest and Vacancy Extensions cover every uncorrected item with no selection or safety confirmation; Best Interest due dates follow the normal Extension rules, Vacancy prints no due date and needs a review inspection at least seven days after mailing. Authoring-16 history documents print every note under the violation, corrected items included. A referred case allows only Update for Case Referral or Compliance Letter; corrections of earlier documents remain possible. Earlier authoring versions are unchanged.

R61 owner clarifications: citation previews and PDFs must contain all four distinct copies per charge; new Best Interest Extensions have no signer block; their missing signer placeholders explain the reported circles. New compliance letters retain item-specific observation histories and explicit Abate values. Correction money formatting never changes its stored units or pricing. The owner confirmed that the existing Best Interest safety-exclusion checkbox remains; new authoring-15 Best Interest requires that explicit confirmation but no separate safety-basis narrative. Older formats and other families retain their requirements.
