# Documentation release narrative (retired text)

Consolidation Phase 7 rewrote the current staff and developer documentation by feature. The passages below are the text it replaced, kept verbatim for provenance: release-by-release narrative, release-labelled format history and the R62 editions of the developer guides. They describe how behavior arrived, not how to use or change the current program; the current documents, CHANGELOG.md and the release reviews in this folder govern. Nothing here is an instruction for current work.

## docs/staff/PROGRAM-MANUAL.md, chapter 2 (compatibility history)

**Compatibility.** This release uses case schema 5, summary version 6, workspace backup version 2 and current authoring version 16; new referrals use item-specific history and Days Outstanding, R62 authoring 16 changes the Best Interest, Vacancy and history-note presentation, while earlier issued formats and existing special-document layouts retain their versions; R54 introduced authoring 12 for the aligned Best Interest and Vacancy Extension layout, and R56 introduced authoring 13 for their required item due dates. Earlier releases predating schema 5 cannot read schema-5 case files. An earlier release does not know a newer authoring layout: before rollback, finish or discard unfinished drafts and corrections, and afterwards rely on the retained original PDFs for documents issued under the newer release. A runtime rollback changes installed files; it does not reverse issued records or restore workspace data. IT must assess data compatibility and retained requests before rollback. Never replace current operational records with an old backup without an approved recovery plan.

## docs/staff/PROGRAM-MANUAL.md, chapter 11

**R54 extension layout.** Newly authored Best Interest and Vacancy Extensions use the regular Extension's header, recipient block, items table, continuation headers, footer and copy handling. Authoring-14 and earlier retain their approved language; R61 simplifies the new Best Interest narrative as described above. All items are listed, including corrected items ruled through with the confirmed date. Extended items show the new due date; unselected items keep the existing date. From R56 (authoring 13), every affected item of a new Best Interest or Vacancy Extension needs a later due date, marked Affected and entered in the violation items table; unaffected items keep their dates. Authoring-12 and earlier drafts keep the Affected outstanding items list in their details. Vacancy Extensions issued before R56 may be conditional: they display “Conditional — see conditions above” for affected items, keep their stored repair dates and reprint unchanged. Authoring-15 and earlier Vacancy Extensions and authoring-14 and earlier Best Interest Extensions retain their supervisory signer after the table; new Best Interest (authoring 15 and later) and Vacancy (authoring 16) Extensions have no signer block. From R62 (authoring 16) both cover every uncorrected item without Affected checkboxes. The regular Extension is unchanged. Previously issued documents and corrections of authoring-11 or earlier documents retain their historical layout.

## docs/staff/PROGRAM-MANUAL.md, release appendices after chapter 22

### Current Workspace and referral improvements

To fill a missing corrective-action detail, activate its fill-in bubble, enter text in Add corrective-action detail and choose Save. Cancel or Escape leaves the bubble unchanged. The dialog returns focus to the item and uses the ordinary draft-saving process.

Citation editors initially show all four distinct Court, Agency, Inspector and Defendant copies per charge. Show representative court copies optionally switches to a compact preview; Show all four citation copies restores the complete preview. Every final citation packet contains all four distinct approved copies for each charge; longer packets may contain additional letter or continuation pages. Verify the issued PDF, not only the compact editor view.

New City Attorney referrals and updates show each item’s recorded inspection observations, including corrected items. Days Outstanding starts at that item’s first observation and stops at correction; an outstanding item uses its latest recorded observation. Later inspections do not increase a corrected item’s duration. Unrelated visits and carried findings are excluded. Missing or inconsistent dates display Unavailable; investigate the source record rather than assuming zero days. Previously issued referrals keep their historical format.

In the editor, mailing addresses flow below the header to prevent overlap when interactive fields expand. Issued PDFs retain their existing approved left-window positioning. Native envelope and printer acceptance remains required.

## R62 document changes

New authoring-16 Referrals for Prosecution, Updates for Case Referral and Compliance Letters print each inspector note under the violation in Corrections Required, as on an Extension, oldest first and with its date, including notes on corrected items. The Inspection History column keeps dates, outcomes, CORRECTED, citations and Days Outstanding, without notes. Long notes continue on the next page; every word prints once. Earlier documents keep notes in the history column.

## R61 document presentation

New authoring-15 referrals and compliance letters show each item's actual inspection history and observation notes. A corrected observation displays its date once, with CORRECTED beneath it. Days Outstanding ends at correction; unrelated or carried observations do not create additional visits. Compliance letters retain Abate Yes/No; missing legacy information is labeled Unavailable rather than invented. Issued histories remain frozen.

Citation correction review displays dollars and cents consistently, including assessment components stored as integer cents. It does not recalculate the recorded correction values. Current order language displays 27.05(2)(g)2 without changing its canonical charge identity or pricing.

The Best Interest signer placeholders are separate from document footers. Removing the new Best Interest signer block removes those bubbles. Existing single-page and continuation footer conventions and approved citation forms are retained. Verify the actual PDF and City print settings; incomplete new citation packets are refused instead of silently filed.

## docs/developer/01-ARCHITECTURE.md (whole R62 edition)

# R62 current architecture

Authoring-12 Best Interest and Vacancy Extensions reuse the regular Extension's layout and item table, with their unchanged language and retained supervisory signer after the table. Conditional vacancy labels (authoring 12 and earlier only) do not alter stored repair dates. From authoring 13 every affected Best Interest or Vacancy item carries a later due date entered in the items table, and Vacancy Extensions are always dated. Authoring-11 and earlier special documents and their corrections retain the historical narrative renderer; the regular Extension output remains unchanged at every authoring version. Publication and correction snapshots retain the selected rendering version.

Writer and Reports are browser applications served by a helper bound to 127.0.0.1. IndexedDB retains private drafts and pending operations; integrity-protected JSON and immutable PDF receipts live in configured operational folders. Reports is read-only against case history. The Reporting Index is a cache: the helper serves a summary only when it equals its projection of the verified case, and writes only summaries that do. No central service is introduced.

Browser startup entries import their core wiring. Descriptively named modules export their real functions directly. state.js owns shared mutable Writer and Reports stores. Cohesive domain services keep private state; pure presentation functions receive explicit, scoped contexts. There is no bridge registry or universal runtime getter table. The proof service retains a small explicit dependency interface for isolated integrity tests.

Writer modules are organized by feature (Consolidation Phase 5, a mechanical move): accela_import.js holds the whole Accela import path, reinspection.js the worksheet and walkthrough, editor_screens.js the document editor, app_screens.js the screen dispatcher, shared modals and case list, and app_core.js shared state, utilities and the global listeners, whose registration order is unchanged. docs/developer/02-CODE-MAP.md lists every module's ownership.

The helper owns case merge/validate/write under per-case locks and conservative pricing serialization. Migration publishes durable intent before a case and registry replacement. Recovery completes only a matching prior/planned revision and retains quarantined bytes. A request-scoped verification context checks event/record chains, migration evidence and protected anchors; it is never a cross-request trust cache. Read-only Reports overlaps at most four file reads in batches of sixteen, with trust decisions on the consuming thread.

PDF receipts bind case/document/frozen-version identity, print-source hash and exact original bytes. Shared coordination precedes producing a new original. Retried filing retrieves the retained bytes; filenames alone do not establish identity. Outbox transfers verify exact bytes and refuse conflicts. Transfer, direct filing and rendering reach a filing folder only through one per-name lock and a publication that never replaces an existing name.

Every non-static helper request requires a random per-start token, with constant-time comparison and existing Host, Origin, fetch-site and JSON guards. helper/server.py lists every API route in GET_ROUTES and POST_ROUTES; do_GET and do_POST apply the token, Host, Origin and fetch-site checks, and for every POST the JSON check, before calling a route's handler, and the session test iterates both tables. A private user-profile bootstrap hands the token to the browser through a fragment that is removed immediately. Public pages disclose no secret. A browser-profile bootstrap credential scoped to the loopback origin and port permits authorized reload after helper restart. Public readiness reveals version and readiness only.

Application CSP authenticates the inline bootstrap and bundle by SHA-256, restricts connections to self and the Reports City GIS origin, and forbids objects, framing by other pages and base overrides. Rendering validates embedded-only resources, inserts a restrictive first-head policy and blocks network resolution in a fresh per-render profile.

The installed layout stays flat under LOCALAPPDATA/MadisonDocWriter. R52 integrity model (no signing key): the installed launcher checks the share against runtime-manifest.json, staging every listed file and rechecking its size and SHA-256; it rejects additions, omissions, symlinks, changes and downgrades (helperBuild and helper version). Run from the share, launch.py first removes its own folder from the import path and hands off to the installed launcher. The desktop icon runs the installed launch.py with the same installation's pythonw.exe: it applies an approved update, starts server.py hidden and detached (or reuses a running helper of the same version), waits for /dw-status, opens Writer in a Chrome/Edge app window in the existing profile and exits; failures are shown in a Windows dialog and logged to logs/launcher.log. Start Document Writer.bat remains the console diagnostic launcher, where the helper runs in the console. Exit Document Writer (Tools & settings) is the only stop path: an authenticated POST /helper/shutdown in POST_ROUTES that refuses with readable reasons (409) while the helper runs another POST, holds a file_lock or finds install-transaction.json, otherwise answers and ends serve_forever so the process exits; a client force flag never bypasses it. The launcher never stops a running helper, closing a window never stops it, and open Writer and Reports windows learn of the stop through a BroadcastChannel. DESKTOP-LAUNCH.md describes the process model, logging and shutdown. Deliberate tampering is guarded by NTFS permissions on the deployment folder (release owner write, inspectors read), which IT verifies before rollout.

The maintained layout is the only source tree. Tools resolve source assets directly and generate derived modules and identity under build/. The deploy manifest resolves maintained paths to stable installed filenames. The trusted installed launcher authenticates the candidate share using installed verification code and the candidate file manifest, with deployment-folder permissions protecting against deliberate replacement; the shared bootstrap performs only a standard-library handoff when a verified installation exists.

R60 authoring-14 referrals retain item-specific inspection observations in each item’s existing hist field. Lineage follows source-document/item IDs, excludes carried findings and unrelated visits, and includes the current worksheet. The display computes civil calendar days from first observation to correction, or latest recorded observation while outstanding; missing or inconsistent dates display Unavailable. Long histories paginate into labeled continuation rows with each observation and note retained. Corrections patch retained visit observations, and issued reprints never query later live case history. Authoring-13 and earlier referral renderers remain unchanged. Case schema, summary and backup versions remain unchanged. New ordinary drafts use authoring 14; the existing special-document families retain authoring 13 because their authored layout is unchanged.

Workspace owns the prominent import/shared-search entry area; import stays a guarded internal route. Reinspection deadline assignment reuses duePanel/assignDue and the existing draft result store, outside collapsed Inspection checks. Corrective-action placeholders use the shared accessible utility dialog with context validation. Citation editors can expand all four copies; final printJob remains a separate complete packet. The R60 screen-only address rule flows the address below an expanding editor header; printableHTML keeps the approved fixed envelope positioning.

R61 introduces authoring 15 for new referral/compliance history presentation and Best Interest Extensions. Compliance history is a presentation capability, not referral status. Older issued authoring versions and their correction renderers remain unchanged. See R61-REVIEW.md for implementation and the confirmed retained Best Interest safety checkbox.

R62 introduces authoring 16. Best Interest and Vacancy Extensions derive special.affected from every uncorrected item (syncAffected) instead of a selection; Best Interest takes the worksheet's Next due date like a normal Extension and Vacancy prints no due date for uncorrected items, keeping source deadlines in originalDues. Authoring-16 history documents render notes under the violation (historyNotesHTML) and pagination continues them like history rows. Import candidates hide status/type categories at parse time and used inspections per case at plan time. The helper mirrors the extension rules (extension_format) and refuses new non-referral documents on a referred case. See R62-REVIEW.md.

## docs/developer/03-DOMAIN-RULES.md (whole R62 edition)

# Domain rules

Legal wording, ordinance interpretation, court schedules, numbering, authority, seasonal policy and language approval belong to the division. The mandatory-appearance hold remains enforced in Writer and helper until approved distinct defendant instructions are supplied. Historical frozen reprints remain unchanged. Current approved document and court wording (notice blocks, document bodies, court details, copies, instructions and contact) is data in app/assets/approved_language.json; change it only with the division's language approval (L5). Issued documents keep their frozen copies.

Preserve issued snapshots, event/item/root/visit identities, action chronology, correction lineage, withdrawals versus compliance, deadline limits, initial notice/citation atomic publication and legacy readers. Device saving is not issuance; a filed marker is not PDF proof; an Outlook draft is not delivery. A pending publication confirms its retained request before another action.

Inspection imports use the unchanged fixed read-only SQL query or division SSRS Excel. Both paths use the inspection-type/status matrix in accela_import.js: Initial Inspection permits Official Notice, Citation, No Occupancy and the two Abatement results; Reinspection permits CA Referral/City Attorney Referral, Compliance, Citation, Extension Processing and No Occupancy; CA Reinspection permits Compliance and Non Compliance. Completed and all unlisted results are refused. Scheduled, Pending, Assist, unknown statuses, unsupported types, missing actual dates and future dates cannot authorize an import. Blank times remain blank. A result status is not evidence that Document Writer issued a document; shared record identities independently prevent reuse. Existing unfinished work opens through Continue existing case. Several eligible results require an explicit choice. Snow Removal record category retains the MGO 10.28 route.

New notices begin with no automatic item deadline. At most three distinct outstanding-item dates are permitted by the existing rules. Physical visits and their item findings are distinct from document actions. No-access findings are not compliance. Withdrawn items retain their reason and identity. Private language edits survive removal/reselection; reviewed-version adoption is explicit; issued wording stays frozen.

Pricing preserves determined locks, counted history, approved basis provenance, required measurements and unchanged-correction prices. Manual authority is separate from the mandatory wording hold. Snow uses its existing site/season rules and no ordinary repair deadline. Do not change policy to bypass a readiness check.

Reports accepts only verified case bytes matching summary sourceRevision, and only a summary whose reportable fields equal the helper's projection of that verified case (helper/case_projection.py, the exact mirror of summarize()); legacy, stale, duplicate, unreadable, unproven or non-matching projections remain visible as excluded coverage. The case file is the authority: a rebuild rewrites summaries, never cases or integrity history. Current table, CSV, workbook and report print use the same row ordering. Cached review metadata is labeled and cannot bypass publication validation.

R60 referral rule confirmed by the product owner: show inspections that actually observed each item, beginning at that item’s first observation, including later-added items. Days Outstanding stops at correction; outstanding items end at their latest recorded observation. Carried compliance is not a new observation. No date is inferred from mailing/issuance or unrelated case visits. Required citation copies remain Court, Agency, Inspector and Defendant, with approved distinct content.

R62 owner rules (Development 3): imports follow the type/status table with No Fee Extension Processing and No Fee No Occupancy; Scheduled, Pending, Completed, Inspection, No Violation, Reinspection, No Fee Reinspection, Initial Inspection No Entry, Assist, undated and already-used inspections are hidden (the refresh of an unissued draft's own inspection is not used); other pairs are listed unavailable. Authoring-16 Best Interest and Vacancy Extensions cover every uncorrected item with no selection or safety confirmation; Best Interest due dates follow the normal Extension rules, Vacancy prints no due date and needs a review inspection at least seven days after mailing. Authoring-16 history documents print every note under the violation, corrected items included. A referred case allows only Update for Case Referral or Compliance Letter; corrections of earlier documents remain possible. Earlier authoring versions are unchanged.

R61 owner clarifications: citation previews and PDFs must contain all four distinct copies per charge; new Best Interest Extensions have no signer block; their missing signer placeholders explain the reported circles. New compliance letters retain item-specific observation histories and explicit Abate values. Correction money formatting never changes its stored units or pricing. The owner confirmed that the existing Best Interest safety-exclusion checkbox remains; new authoring-15 Best Interest requires that explicit confirmation but no separate safety-basis narrative. Older formats and other families retain their requirements.

## docs/developer/04-PITFALLS.md (whole R62 edition)

# R62 current pitfalls

- Keep authoring-11 and earlier special extensions and their corrections on the historical renderer. Do not downgrade authoring-12 drafts during pricing refresh, publication or correction. Conditional vacancy labels are presentation, not replacement repair dates, and exist only for authoring-12 and earlier reprints; never create an authoring-13 conditional Vacancy Extension.

- Never skip integrity/history/publication checks, regenerate genesis, overwrite protected anchors or publish a migration registry ahead of a recoverable case write.
- Never infer orphaned-lock ownership from age. Recovery requires the inspected token, verified stopped ownership, named administrator and reason.
- Never change a charge, date, time or wording merely to bypass a refusal. Keep the mandatory wording hold and immutable historical sources.
- Never regenerate a PDF original after a lost acknowledgement when a retained operation receipt exists. Verify case/document/version/source and exact bytes.
- Never claim a pending browser write is saved, treat unknown outbox/health as zero, silently overwrite a conflict or replace current typed work with a delayed response.
- Import eligibility must use the defined result table, not a literal Completed-only comparison. Never treat a status label as proof of prior program issuance or allow an unknown result silently.
- Never trust shared configuration to select a new outbound host, SQL server or integrity anchor. Change the allowlist in trusted-connections.json in a new release built by the release owner.
- Never obtain a session token from a public page or include it in logs, reports or shared files. Keep user-profile bootstrap access private; reload through the original profile after restart.
- Never run a shared launcher as the root of trust for its own first installation. Check the initial deploy ZIP SHA-256 against the release owner’s independently published digest, then use the locally installed verified shortcut.
- Never put generated source, developer dependencies, historical development prompts or operational records in deployment packages.
- Never run concurrent Chromium jobs during a measured browser performance gate. JSDOM cannot prove layout, SQL text equality cannot prove City connectivity, and mocked Outlook cannot prove native delivery.

- Name UTF-8 for every helper and tool text read or write and every text subprocess. The Windows default code page is not UTF-8; audit_utf8_io refuses implicit encodings. Keep strict decoding: never accept BOM, UTF-16 or replacement characters in case or integrity data.
- File PDF originals only through server.file_pdf_bytes. It holds the per-name "issued-pdf" lock, compares bytes and publishes with publish_new, which never replaces a name. Never add another lock key, an existence check outside that lock or a replacing write for a filing folder; remove an outbox copy only after the shared copy reads back identical.
- Archive only an approved path-to-SHA-256 set through release_inputs.archive. Never traverse a staging folder for archive content or verify it through an excluding fingerprint.
- Regenerate manual editions only with tools/build_manual.py, which stages, validates and replaces all three together. Never hand-edit or copy a single edition; every edition carries the current source digest.

- summarize() in workspace_shell.js and helper/case_projection.py are one projection in two languages. Change them together, with the mirrored tables; audit_case_projection compares them over generated and captured real cases. Never accept a cached summary field the projection of the verified case does not reproduce, and never repair a summary by editing the case. Compare summary values as canonical JSON (case_projection.same_json, RFC 8785), never with Python ==, which treats false as 0 and true as 1 where Reports does not.
- Policy scripts follow the same explicit UTF-8 rule as helper/ and tools/; audit_utf8_io scans all three.

- The manifest catches mistakes and damaged copies, not someone who can rewrite both the files and the manifest. Never weaken the deployment folder’s NTFS permissions (release owner write, inspectors read).

R61 introduces authoring 15 for new referral/compliance history presentation and Best Interest Extensions. Compliance history is a presentation capability, not referral status. Older issued authoring versions and their correction renderers remain unchanged. See R61-REVIEW.md for implementation and the confirmed retained Best Interest safety checkbox.

R62: keep authoring-15 Best Interest drafts and authoring-13 Vacancy drafts on their formats; only new drafts and explicit adoption use authoring 16. Scoped orders keep authoring 13. Do not re-add a stylesheet rule for authoring-16 history notes: the approved print style hides it-note on corrected rows, so those notes carry an explicit display. ## docs/staff/NEW-DOCUMENTS-GUIDE.md

**Best Interest Extension.** A new Best Interest Extension (R62, authoring 16) extends every uncorrected item. Enter Reasons for Additional Time. Due dates work as on a normal Extension: enter each outstanding item's Next due date on the reinspection worksheet; you can change them in Violation items. There are no Affected checkboxes, no safety confirmation and no signer block. Earlier Best Interest documents and drafts keep their format. It is not a City Attorney referral.

**Vacancy Extension.** A new Vacancy Extension (R62, authoring 16) extends every uncorrected item while the building or apartment / unit remains vacant. Enter Scope and the Next scheduled review inspection, at least seven days after the mailing date; contact-by is calculated seven calendar days before it. Uncorrected items print no due date; corrected items keep their dates. There are no Affected checkboxes, safety, request, vacancy-basis, conditions, expiration, transfer, short-notice or authority/signer fields, and no signer block. Earlier Vacancy Extensions reprint unchanged. Renewal requires a new action. Apartment scope preserves common-area duties.
